# ONIMACHAIN — 12-Second Animated Peptide Explainers

Production-ready dialogue and Seedance prompts for 15 high-interest products in the current OnimaChain collection. This is a priority production list, not a measured sales ranking.

## Master format

- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 portrait
- **Master size:** 1080 × 1350 pixels
- **Phone version:** 720 × 900 pixels
- **Length:** exactly 12 seconds
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps
- **Composition:** keep faces, molecules, and important action inside the center 70%; keep the lower 20% visually quiet for captions
- **Export:** H.264 MP4, `yuv420p`, web optimized
- **Audio:** calm, natural voice; no sales-announcer delivery
- **Captions:** add clean captions after generation; do not ask the video model to draw readable text

### Shared Seedance style

Append this to every scene prompt:

> Premium cinematic 3D animated explainer, one relatable adult character, deep obsidian environment, liquid-glass layers, a scientifically inspired molecule as the visual focus, restrained neon rim light, tactile molecular texture, slow deliberate camera motion, clear visual metaphor, realistic depth and shadows, no logos, no labels, no generated text, no needles, no dosing, no before-and-after body transformation, no superhero effects. 4:5 portrait, 1080×1350, exactly 12 seconds. Leave the lower 20% calm for captions.

## Timing template

- **0–2 seconds:** Introduce the character and the problem or body system.
- **2–9 seconds:** Show the peptide signal or research idea visually.
- **9–12 seconds:** Hold on the molecule and finish the dialogue.

## Production guardrails

- These explainers describe what each product is known or studied for. They do not promise results.
- Do not show dosing, injection technique, medical instructions, dramatic body changes, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Keep approved uses narrow and accurate. Do not visually imply that experimental products are approved medicines.
- Use the dialogue as voiceover and as editor-added captions. The existing website player autoplays muted.
- The current OnimaChain encoder trims videos to **10 seconds**. Before importing these 12-second masters, change the limit in `tools/encode-videos.mjs` from `Math.min(10, ...)` to `Math.min(12, ...)`, or create a separate 10-second website cut.
