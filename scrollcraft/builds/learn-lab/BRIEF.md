# OnimaChain Learning Lab brief

This is an in-place redesign of the existing `/learn` route, not a new website. Supplied answers come from the user's Learn Page Redesign brief; decisions not specified there are marked **Authored**.

## Creative authority and eight topics

1. **Vibe (supplied):** interactive science museum, premium learning platform, OnimaChain visual system. **Authored:** precise, luminous, curious, dimensional.
2. **Journey (supplied):** hero explaining purpose; why come here; differentiated lessons in four categories; optional beginner path; educational philosophy; paths back into the product.
3. **Energy (supplied):** animated, deep and moving, but useful to beginners and researchers. **Authored:** peak in the hero claim transformation, calmer reading through the catalog.
4. **Feeling and peak (authored from supplied lesson 01):** curiosity, recognition, agency, exploration, clarity. Peak: “I moved a claim forward and watched the study's limits disappear.”
5. **Signature (authored from supplied visual):** an operable claim-transmission display that changes context layers as a five-stage conceptual claim moves from study to viral post.
6. **Aesthetic range (supplied):** science museum plus premium learning platform, not ten uniform cards. **Authored:** luminous technical editorial, with varied exhibit composition and restrained 3D cues.
7. **World structure (authored):** distinct exhibits, not one continuous camera move; the reader can filter and jump.
8. **Assets (existing):** OnimaChain logo/palette, existing live MutationLadder and research data, local typefaces. No new image generation or footage.

## Subject, audience, job

- **Subject (supplied):** peptide research literacy and how claims mutate.
- **Audience (supplied):** beginners, students, customers, researchers, and company staff.
- **Single job (supplied):** teach visitors to read peptide research without being fooled by missing context.
- **Belief by the end (supplied):** “Peptide research is complicated. Understanding it does not have to be.”
- **Next action (supplied):** pick a lesson, or use learning to explore compounds, the Watchlist, or a claim.

## Token plan

- Canvas `#081019`, raised surface `#132330`, ink `#E9F7F5`, muted ink `#A8C0C6`, laboratory cyan `#69DFF2`, caution amber `#F4BE7A`. Lesson exhibit hues vary deliberately because the brief explicitly requires distinct neon accents.
- Display and reading copy: Manrope Variable; occasional exhibit title: Unbounded Variable; instrument labels: JetBrains Mono. All are existing local fonts.
- Layout: asymmetric editorial exhibits inside four category chapters; one wide live feature, tall and compact exhibit shapes, filtered in place.
- Signature: the claim-transmission instrument, not generic hover tilt. The rest of the motion is subordinate.

## Grammar, journey, and feeling curve

**Grammar: research exhibit index.** It adapts gallery/catalog to an educational route: category index, operable lesson objects, and a quiet exit into the site's research tools. It retains the existing global navigation. A full-screen video scrub, fixed cinematic pin, or continuous fly-through would distract from reading and duplicate the homepage hero.

1. **Curiosity:** a spatial claim display is already alive next to the clear page purpose.
2. **Recognition:** three concise columns name what goes wrong between study and claim.
3. **Agency:** a visitor can choose a category or optional beginner path.
4. **Exploration:** distinct lesson exhibits reveal topic-specific diagrams and honest availability states.
5. **Clarity:** a quiet philosophy statement resolves to three useful product destinations.

**Peak:** the hero's five-stage conceptual claim display, operated by pointer, touch, or keyboard. No published-study wording is invented for this schematic; the real sourced example remains in the live lesson.

**Tell-someone sentence:** “It's the page where you move a peptide claim from study to viral post and watch the missing context fall away.”

## Layer contract

| Plane | Content | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far | faint lab grid and soft specimen light | static, with category-specific background changes | never undercuts the headline |
| Mid | claim-stage rail and fine connection traces | state-driven, not autoplay | represents an illustrative process |
| Focal | example wording and contextual annotation | change as the slider advances | no false source quotation |
| Near | topic-specific exhibit diagrams | subtle, distinct loops and scroll entry | reduced-motion alternative is static |
| Copy | semantic heading, explanation, actions | stable | CTA visible in the opening viewport |

## Verification targets

- Desktop, 390px phone, compact phone, reduced motion, and intermediate scroll states.
- Category filters, optional path, live lesson navigation, preview teasers, and sources.
- No empty preview destinations, no horizontal overflow, keyboard focus visible, no console errors.
- The existing live MutationLadder and its verified study links remain functional.
