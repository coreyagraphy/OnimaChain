# Cyravon homepage hero brief

Reused from the user's supplied direction and the existing Cyravon build. This is a focused revision of the production homepage hero, not a new standalone site.

## Creative direction

1. **Vibe:** bold, neon, layered, immersive, liquid, dimensional. The reference is the existing glowing molecular world, pushed into a clearer spatial journey.
2. **Journey:** begin with the BPC-157 molecule slowly turning; carry the camera through the molecule as the visitor scrolls; clear the structure; then reveal the plain-language commerce message and shopping actions.
3. **Energy curve:** quiet curiosity at rest, acceleration through the molecule, then a calm and readable landing.
4. **Feeling and peak:** curiosity becomes immersion, then clarity. The peak is: "I scrolled through a peptide and the shopping message came into focus on the other side."
5. **Signature move:** the molecular threshold. Scroll changes the visitor from outside observer to moving through the chain, then the molecule becomes the frame around the final message.
6. **Aesthetic range:** maximal neon depth with controlled typography. The hero gets the spectacle; the final message stays quiet enough to read.
7. **World structure:** one continuous 3D microjourney inside the hero, resolving into the existing commerce page below.
8. **Existing assets:** the sequence-derived BPC-157 model, current WebGL renderer, particle fields, lighting rig, poster fallbacks, and Cyravon palette.

## Subject, audience, and job

- **Subject:** a commerce collection of commonly discussed peptides with checked sources attached.
- **Audience:** a first-time shopper who may know a peptide name but not the science vocabulary.
- **Single job:** make the collection feel desirable and help the visitor shop without making them decode laboratory language.
- **Belief by the end:** Cyravon makes peptide products easier to explore and keeps the source material available when I want it.
- **Next action:** Shop the collection.

## Grammar

**Molecular threshold:** a bespoke hero passage that opens into a gallery/catalog page. It forbids an opening headline over the molecule, repeated pinned story acts, technical system-state labels, and a blank final frame.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Content | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far | deep gradient and star field | slowest lateral drift | establishes scale |
| Mid | violet dust and in-world text | moderate drift | appears only after the camera enters |
| Subject | BPC-157 molecular chain | idle rotation, then camera fly-through | remains the clear opening focus |
| Near | haze and passing residues | fastest parallax | creates visible occlusion without hiding final copy |
| Copy | real HTML headline and actions | hidden until the camera clears the chain | fully readable and held at the resolved exit |

## Feeling curve

1. **Curiosity:** the molecule turns in a quiet field with no large headline competing with it.
2. **Immersion:** scrolling moves the camera between glowing residues and bonds.
3. **Understanding:** three short, plain-language labels explain studies, online claims, and unknowns.
4. **Confidence:** the scientific labels clear and the commerce headline settles in with one obvious next action.

## Verification targets

- Opening, midpoint, and resolved exit on desktop.
- Opening and resolved exit at 390 x 844.
- Reduced motion shows the final message immediately and removes the pinned travel.
- No visible technical phrases such as "no corpus" or "source access."
- No horizontal overflow, console errors, or unreachable controls.
